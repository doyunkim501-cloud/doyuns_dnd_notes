One of the most infamous [[World - Races/Humans\|Humans]], Mad Mage Makarr rose to eminence most disreputable when [[World - Religions/The Eternal Sun/The God Emperor (Sol)\|The God Emperor (Sol)]] was but a mortal. He was the founding principal of the [[Guilds/Arcane University (North)\|Arcane University (North)]] in [[World - Geography - Material Plane/The Imperial Continent/Chrannoth-Uggan (Northern Capitol)\|Chrannoth-Uggan (Northern Capitol)]], and indeed was responsible for the great northern capitol's founding. During the [[Lore - Foundatiaonal/Cataclysm\|Cataclysm]], Makarr betrayed his friend [[World - Religions/The Eternal Sun/The God Emperor (Sol)\|The God Emperor (Sol)]] and left him to fight the [[Villains/Demons/Archdemon Krintar\|Archdemon Krintar]]. 

Makarr was born to humble origins. His father was a low-ranking member of the [[Guilds/Guild of Blades\|Guild of Blades]] who never amounted to much in missions. His mother was a bartender in [[World - Geography - Material Plane/The Imperial Continent/Chrannoth-Uggan (Northern Capitol)\|Chrannoth-Uggan (Northern Capitol)]]. The family had little to their name, but provided little Makarr with food on the table and a warm roof over his head. 

As Makarr grew in stature, he also found renown beyond his parents. [[World - Metaphysics/The Weave\|The Weave]] oft whispered to Makarr, which let him understand the workings of magic beyond his peers. In his teens, Makarr left on a quest to slay a [[World - Monsters/Dragons/Red Dragon\|Red Dragon]], which his peers thought was an ordeal beyond him. 

Guided by [[World - Metaphysics/The Weave\|The Weave]]'s voice, Makarr journeyed to the [[Fire Mountain - College of Magic\|Fire Mountain - College of Magic]]]], before the [[Guilds/College of Magic (South)\|College of Magic (South)]] was founded. Here he met a young [[World - Religions/The Eternal Sun/The God Emperor (Sol)\|The God Emperor (Sol)]], who was also guided by [[World - Metaphysics/The Weave\|The Weave]] on this mission. The two young men joined forces and after a furious battle, brought down the Red Dragon within its lair. 

Thereafter, Makarr gained infamy as a loose cannon within the [[Guilds/Arcane University (North)\|Arcane University (North)]]. He quickly rose to power as the University's Headmaster, lending support to Sol. Makarr also rallied the support of the overlooked Races, ensuring a peace treaty with the [[World - Races/Goblins\|Goblins]], [[World - Races/Kobolds\|Kobolds]], and [[World - Races/Centaurs\|Centaurs]]. This brought great stability to the [[World - Geography - Material Plane/The Imperial Continent/The Northern Wall\|The Northern Wall]] and its surrounding regions, enabling routine trade with the [[World - Races/Dwarves\|Dwarves]]. 

However, the Weave ever seeks balance. For all the light that Makarr and Sol did, there too must be dark. 

As Sol began walking [[World - Metaphysics/The Steps of Ascension\|The Steps of Ascension]], the Weave recognized that His brilliance would have no counterweight. Thus, it guided Makarr to seek immortality in a different way. He was urged to carry out foul experiments, all in the name of overcoming [[World - Races/Demons\|Demons]] with as much haste as possible. In doing so, the Weave promised Makarr that he would be able to learn the secret of immortality. The stronger his creations became, the more he would understand of himself. 

Thus, Makarr created beasts most foul and heretic. He released the [[World - Monsters/Glyphid\|Glyphid]]s into deep crevices and mines to see how they would reproduce and mutate their tough carapaces. He took several Dragons and tortured them into becoming [[World - Monsters/Dracolich\|Dracolich]]es. He released a [[World - Monsters/Kraken\|Kraken]] into [[World - Geography - Material Plane/The Imperial Continent/The Western Seas\|The Western Seas]], infesting the northern waters with large tentacles. He created the [[World - Monsters/Sand Wurm\|Sand Wurm]] to terrify [[World - Geography - Material Plane/The Imperial Continent/The Desert Unending\|The Desert Unending]], bringing an end to many of its lush oases. And into the [[World - Geography - Material Plane/The Imperial Continent/Eastern Sea - Ocean Eternal\|Eastern Sea - Ocean Eternal]] Makarr released the [[World - Monsters/Depth Strider\|Depth Strider]], which haunted sailors' dreams for centuries to come. 

Makarr was fundamentally a good man. These experiments, driven by desperation to support his friend Sol, broke him one small step at a time. By the age of 40, when the [[Lore - Foundatiaonal/Cataclysm\|Cataclysm]] began in earnest, as [[Villains/Demons/Demontide\|Demontide]] reached landfall, Makarr was a bitter, twisted man, broken beyond all recognition. The Weave had successfully torn his soul into seven pieces, and he now had little recourse left but to become an [[World - Monsters/Archlich\|Archlich]].

At the plains of [[World - Geography - Material Plane/The Imperial Continent/Urdak-Kahn\|Urdak-Kahn]], as [[Villains/Demons/Demontide\|Demontide]] rushed upon its mighty Mithril walls, [[World - Religions/The Eternal Sun/The God Emperor (Sol)\|The God Emperor (Sol)]], armed with nothing but his Sword of Night and Flame, having sent the entire population away under guard of [[World - Religions/The Eternal Sun/The Divine Generals\|The Divine Generals]], called through The Weave to Makarr to fight alongside him. And at the plains of [[World - Geography - Material Plane/The Imperial Continent/Urdak-Kahn\|Urdak-Kahn]], Makarr showed his old friend his back and journeyed north. 

As the Emperor engaged in ruinous battle and smote the [[Villains/Demons/Archdemon Krintar\|Archdemon Krintar]] into the ground, Makarr landed in [[World - Geography - Material Plane/The Imperial Continent/Chrannoth-Uggan (Northern Capitol)\|Chrannoth-Uggan (Northern Capitol)]]. He walked through secret passageways deep underground and called forth the [[World - Geography - Material Plane/The Imperial Continent/Mirrormere\|Mirrormere]], into which he poured the essence of his humanity - his fears. And in doing so, Makarr became an [[World - Monsters/Archlich\|Archlich]], trapping his soul in seven artefacts of great power. 

Its work completed, the Weave was content to leave the Material Plane under the mere influence of [[Lore - Foundatiaonal/The Dark\|The Dark]]. The Weave whispered to [[World - Religions/The Eternal Sun/The God Emperor (Sol)\|The God Emperor (Sol)]], who had now finished [[World - Metaphysics/The Steps of Ascension\|The Steps of Ascension]] and burned with the power of the Sun. It gave him Makarr's location, and brought the two men to battle. 

After several days, Makarr was sealed away and his body thrown into the [[World - Geography - Material Plane/The Imperial Continent/Eastern Sea - Ocean Eternal\|Eastern Sea - Ocean Eternal]]. As part of this rite, his [[Seven Phylacteries\|Seven Phylacteries]] were also rendered dormant, awaiting the day that Makarr's essence be released into the world again.
