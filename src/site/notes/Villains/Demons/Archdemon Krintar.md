The greatest of the Demons, Krintar was brought forth by [[Lore - Foundatiaonal/The Dark\|The Dark]] to give Demonkind a chance against [[World - Religions/The Eternal Sun/The God Emperor (Sol)\|The God Emperor (Sol)]]. He stood above the authority of [[Villains/Demons/The Nine Circles of Hell\|The Nine Circles of Hell]], ruling even the Nine Demon Princes with an iron fist. 

It was said that Krintar represented [[Lore - Foundatiaonal/The Dark\|The Dark]]'s will made material. He was the bringer of the [[Lore - Foundatiaonal/Cataclysm\|Cataclysm]], ripping open the barrier between worlds with ease. He brought forth armies most unholy, wreaking havoc on the Imperial Continent. 

His greatest creation was [[Villains/Demons/Demontide\|Demontide]], which he unleashed on [[World - Religions/The Eternal Sun/The God Emperor (Sol)\|The God Emperor (Sol)]] in a desperate attempt to end the Anathema of Demons. Despite this mighty attempt, Sol broke [[Villains/Demons/Demontide\|Demontide]] as it smashed through the gates of [[World - Geography - Material Plane/The Imperial Continent/Urdak-Kahn\|Urdak-Kahn]], which completed [[World - Metaphysics/The Steps of Ascension\|The Steps of Ascension]] and lifted Sol's soul to godhood. 

Krintar then drew his eight blades against the God Emperor and engaged him in a single duel. After their bout, Krintar lay buried deep under [[World - Geography - Material Plane/The Imperial Continent/Urdak-Kahn\|Urdak-Kahn]], his essence trapped to the Material Plane by [[Items/the Sword of Night and Flame\|the Sword of Night and Flame]]. 

To his credit, Krintar's essence was so powerful that even a god cannot kill him in full. Thus, he still lies deep beneath the remnant ruins of [[World - Geography - Material Plane/The Imperial Continent/Urdak-Kahn\|Urdak-Kahn]], waiting for the day that a witless mortal chooses to release him from this fate.
