A great amalgamation of Demonkind, this strange entity was created by [[Villains/Demons/Archdemon Krintar\|Archdemon Krintar]] in his desperate pursuit of [[World - Religions/The Eternal Sun/The God Emperor (Sol)\|The God Emperor (Sol)]]'s demise. It wove together the essence of 7,000 Demons in an attempt to give rise to a single entity that could overcome divine power. 

As Demontide broke through [[World - Geography - Material Plane/The Imperial Continent/Urdak-Kahn\|Urdak-Kahn]]'s great gates, it was met with [[World - Religions/The Eternal Sun/The God Emperor (Sol)\|The God Emperor (Sol)]]'s mighty blade of [[Items/the Sword of Night and Flame\|the Sword of Night and Flame]], and the 7,000 essences were released back to [[Lore - Foundatiaonal/The Dark\|The Dark]].
