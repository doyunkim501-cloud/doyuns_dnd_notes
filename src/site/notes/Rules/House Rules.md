Crit (nat 20): Double all damage sources (all damage dice + modifiers, then multiply by 2). 

Using a potion on yourself: Bonus Action, NOT full action. 

Using a potion on another person: Full action. 

Carry weight/encumbrance: NOT tracked. But don't abuse this. 

Flanking in combat: Two allied creatures must be directly opposite each other from their target (x-O-x). This provides Advantage on all attacks. 

Inspiration: May be granted at the DM's discretion. Inspiration provides a free re-roll on a D20. It cannot be used multiple times for one roll. Inspiration may be "traded" between party members. Only a maximum of 10 total Inspiration "charges" can be held by the party at any given time.

During Session Roles
Lore Keeper: ONE party member who volunteers to take notes and summarize the previous session is the Lore Keeper for the active session. This player receives advantage on all skill checks that go to the history/backstory of a region/character/event. 

Loot Goblin: IF the party decides to have a dedicated "banker", that person will also have advantage on skill checks involving trades or transactions. They may also be targeted by enemies who figure out this role, however. 

Navigator: IF the party decides to have a dedicated "navigator", that person will have advantage on navigation rolls unto perpetuity. Assigning a Navigator also means that in normal Travel circumstances, the party is guaranteed to find sufficient edible food (environment zones or specific story conditions providing exceptions).
