Here begins the Great Work, titled "The Imperial Founding: A Treatise of [[World - Religions/The Eternal Sun/The God Emperor (Sol)\|The God Emperor (Sol)]]'s Divinity." This text is intended to record the truth of this world, founded by The God Emperor and protected by His Grace. 

. - [[Key NPCs/Ethain Bibblesqueak\|Ethain Bibblesqueak]]

"Long may the Sun give nourishment and life."

Chapter 1

For eons before [[World - Religions/The Eternal Sun/The God Emperor (Sol)\|The God Emperor (Sol)]] found the Material Plane there was naught but [[Lore - Foundatiaonal/The Dark\|The Dark]]. Fear [[Lore - Foundatiaonal/The Dark\|The Dark]] for it brings treachery and deceit. [[Lore - Foundatiaonal/The Dark\|The Dark]] enwrapped the many Planes with its miasma, giving rise to only [[World - Races/Demons\|Demons]]. The [[World - Religions/Pantheons\|Pantheons]] crafted many Planes to serve their chosen, but there was no world to be home to all mortalkind. 

In the Time Before Time, [[World - Religions/The Eternal Sun/The God Emperor (Sol)\|The God Emperor (Sol)]] saw fit to give birth to the Sun. He brought Light to the Dark, revealing the barren bones of the world. And our God saw fit also to furnish the Material Plane with details aplomb, so that His subjects may live comfortable and meaningful lives. 

To the South he erected the [[Fire Mountain - College of Magic\|Fire Mountain - College of Magic]]. To the North he banished the cold, in the [[Frozen Wastes\|Frozen Wastes]]. To the East, he flooded the [[World - Geography - Material Plane/The Imperial Continent/Eastern Sea - Ocean Eternal\|Eastern Sea - Ocean Eternal]]. To the West he gave the [[World - Geography - Material Plane/The Imperial Continent/The Western Seas\|The Western Seas]]. 

He chose to set the birthplace of the Empire at the plains of [[World - Geography - Material Plane/The Imperial Continent/Urdak-Kahn\|Urdak-Kahn]], in the heart of the Imperial Continent. Here, atop the ruins of the ancient Dwarven homeland, our God raised mighty walls of Mithril and crafted the [[World - Geography - Material Plane/The Imperial Continent/Three Divine Pillars\|Three Divine Pillars]]. Here he raised the [[World - Metaphysics/Barrier of Worlds\|Barrier of Worlds]] so that we may fend off [[Lore - Foundatiaonal/The Dark\|The Dark]] for time eternal. 

"So is the order of the bones of the world; and so was His command." 

Chapter 2

"A God can only be worshipped; but an Emperor may rule." So saith our God as he surveyed the world he had made. He saw that it was yet empty and in need of servants to tend to its matters. 

With a wave of His mighty hand our God opened portals between  the [[World - Metaphysics/Barrier of Worlds\|Barrier of Worlds]]. He gave a good home to his children, [[World - Races/Humans\|Humans]]. He saw fit also to give a home to [[World - Races/Elves\|Elves]]. The [[World - Races/Dwarves\|Dwarves]] already had a home here, as their patrons amongst the [[World - Religions/Pantheons\|Pantheons]] had snuck them into the bones of the world. 

To the small and meek, the Emperor also opened his heart and home. [[World - Races/Gnomes\|Gnomes]] and [[World - Races/Halflings\|Halflings]] were granted reprieve; the [[World - Races/Centaurs\|Centaurs]] in their woodlands wisdoms also; and even the brutish [[World - Races/Orcs\|Orcs]]. Truly, our God was merciful, and our God was kind. 

To see his world order fulfilled, our God stepped down and took on a mortal body. In this form, as Sol, He took the [[World - Religions/The Eternal Sun/The Golden Throne\|The Golden Throne]] in [[World - Geography - Material Plane/The Imperial Continent/Urdak-Kahn\|Urdak-Kahn]]. In doing so, He gave rise to the [[Empire of the Eternal Sun\|Empire of the Eternal Sun]]. He created a merciful world where all races could live in harmony. 

"Fear [[Lore - Foundatiaonal/The Dark\|The Dark]], for it ever seeks to encroach."

Chapter 3

But all was not well. Wicked beings, the [[World - Races/Mindflayers\|Mindflayers]], stepped into the Material Plane. Heretics worshipping false gods not even celebrated by other Races, they waged war from their foul airships. The Mindflayers rained fire and terror down upon the Great Three Cities - [[World - Geography - Material Plane/The Imperial Continent/Urdak-Kahn\|Urdak-Kahn]], [[World - Geography - Material Plane/The Imperial Continent/Chrannoth-Uggan (Northern Capitol)\|Chrannoth-Uggan (Northern Capitol)]], and [[World - Geography - Material Plane/The Imperial Continent/Solaris (Southern Capitol)\|Solaris (Southern Capitol)]]. 

The Mindflayers' cruel campaign was such that the Emperor needed an army to stand against their forces. To this end he raised [[The Imperial Legions\|The Imperial Legions]], a force of mortals, to fight back against the betentacled invaders. To lead the Legions, He bestowed Divine Power upon  [[World - Religions/The Eternal Sun/The Divine Generals\|The Divine Generals]], four mortals in whom he saw great potential. 

Led by these mighty warriors, the Emperor fought back the Mindflayers 'til at last they sued for peace. Thus it was that the Mindflayers were relegated to [[World - Geography - Material Plane/The Imperial Continent/The Underdark\|The Underdark]], trapped in their mad catacombs and labyrinths. 

Countless other invasions came; countless other invasions were beaten back. 

"The Light of the Sun shines radiant over all."

Chapter 4

The [[Lore - Foundatiaonal/Cataclysm\|Cataclysm]] was a necessary tragedy. The Light of the Emperor shone as an incandescent beacon in [[Lore - Foundatiaonal/The Dark\|The Dark]], which sought to devour this World. 

It called forth the Nemesis - [[Villains/Demons/Archdemon Krintar\|Archdemon Krintar]] in an desperate and futile attempt to end the Emperor's light. From the [[Frozen Wastes\|Frozen Wastes]] came Krintar's worst creation - [[Villains/Demons/Demontide\|Demontide]]. As it made landfall, much of the [[World - Races/Dwarves\|Dwarves]] were forced underground once again, out in the [[World - Geography - Material Plane/The Imperial Continent/The Northern Wall\|The Northern Wall]], to take refuge. 

Seeing this calamity befall His loyal subjects, The Emperor felt righteous wrath take root in him. He swore to destroy this threat for good. He directed his Legions to waylay the foul [[World - Races/Demons\|Demons]] so that he could ready [[World - Geography - Material Plane/The Imperial Continent/Urdak-Kahn\|Urdak-Kahn]] for a mighty battle. 

In a moment of divine brilliance, the Emperor descended from [[World - Religions/The Eternal Sun/The Golden Throne\|The Golden Throne]], which he had moved to [[World - Geography - Material Plane/The Imperial Continent/Solaris (Southern Capitol)\|Solaris (Southern Capitol)]] in readiness for such a calamity. He shone his light bright into both [[World - Metaphysics/The Weave\|The Weave]] and [[Lore - Foundatiaonal/The Dark\|The Dark]], calling [[Villains/Demons/Demontide\|Demontide]] to him. With a blasphemous hunger the monstrosity, with [[Villains/Demons/Archdemon Krintar\|Archdemon Krintar]] riding atop, hastened to the Capitol where the Emperor smote it deep into the roots of the world, and the Nemesis with it. 

So came the [[Lore - Foundatiaonal/Cataclysm\|Cataclysm]], and so it ended. 

"There is no darkness that can swallow the light."

Chapter 5

In the wake of the [[Lore - Foundatiaonal/Cataclysm\|Cataclysm]] the Emperor fortified his Empire. He called forth craftsmen, warriors, scholars, and troubadours. Thus came the Guilds, ordering the workers of the world. 

The [[Guilds/Guild of Lore\|Guild of Lore]] governs the storytellers, singers, and missive-bearers. They must furnish the morale of the Empire so that His loyal subjects can toil without heavy hearts. 

The [[Guilds/Guild of Crafts\|Guild of Crafts]] is the economical backbone of the Empire. Without a well-ordered mercantile body, it is impossible to maintain any quality of life. 

The [[Guilds/Guidl of Tinkerers\|Guidl of Tinkerers]] is the intellectual body of the Empire. Their inventions ensure that new creations constantly enter the market and that His subjects do not fall behind others' prowess. 

The [[Guilds/Guild of Blades\|Guild of Blades]] keeps order outside of [[The Imperial Legions\|The Imperial Legions]]. Where common folk need protection and arms, the Guild is there to keep them safe. In modern days, the Guild of Blades is much akin to the [[Adventurer's Guild\|Adventurer's Guild]], a common and crude creation that governs naught but contracts of those who cannot maintain gainful employment. 

The [[Guilds/Guild of Shadows\|Guild of Shadows]] governs the seedy underbelly of the Empire. There is no doubt of the loyalty of His subjects; but mortalkind is flawed. Thus, a structure such as this is needed to keep the pickpockets, thieves, common thugs, and assassins in check so as to avoid harm to the general populace. 

The [[Guilds/Guild of Crafts\|Guild of Crafts]] is the all-important governing body of the artisans who are responsible for the majority of the goods and services that keep the good Empire running. 

The [[Guilds/Arcane University (North)\|Arcane University (North)]] and the [[Guilds/College of Magic (South)\|College of Magic (South)]] serve distinct functions. In the North, the [[Guilds/Arcane University (North)\|Arcane University (North)]] is pledged to the pure pursuit of arcane knowledge; to the South, the [[Guilds/College of Magic (South)\|College of Magic (South)]] trains the battlemages of [[The Imperial Legions\|The Imperial Legions]] such that His military might is well-represented on every field. 

"I am the shield of His faith, and I am the spear of His belief."

Chapter 6

After the world was ordered well and saved from the [[Lore - Foundatiaonal/Cataclysm\|Cataclysm]] the Emperor moved the seat of power to [[World - Geography - Material Plane/The Imperial Continent/Solaris (Southern Capitol)\|Solaris (Southern Capitol)]], closer to the [[Fire Mountain - College of Magic\|Fire Mountain - College of Magic]]. Here, he rules with the assistance of the [[World - Religions/The Eternal Sun/Church of the White Flame (Eternal Flame)\|Church of the White Flame (Eternal Flame)]] and [[World - Religions/The Eternal Sun/The Divine Generals\|The Divine Generals]]. 

This is the order of the world, and this is the world that is ordered. 

"His love knows no bounds for we, his children."
