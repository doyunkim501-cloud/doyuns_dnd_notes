The Three Phylacteries of [[Villains/Mad Mage Makarr\|Mad Mage Makarr]] were crafted the Mage himself as part of his Rite of Ascension. Makarr delved long and deep into the great mysteries of the Weave and came face to face with its aspect as [[Lore - Foundatiaonal/The Dark\|The Dark]]. The Phylacteries were created in moments where Makarr was forced to grapple with his humanity itself and overstep the bounds of simple humanity, performing depraved acts. These seven acts are otherwise known in the orthodoxy of the [[World - Religions/The Eternal Sun/Church of the White Flame (Eternal Flame)\|Church of the White Flame (Eternal Flame)]] as "The Seven Betrayals". 

1. The First Betrayal was the simplest. As Makarr journeyed in the north, he came across a certain logging town. The town was fed in large part by a farmstead on its outskirts. Makarr was offered lodging by the humble farmer family on a dark and stormy night. A simple dispute arose when Makarr identified himself as a great mage, for the townsfolk in those times feared the arcane. That night, Makarr rose in the middle of the night, hearing voices. The farmer and his wife were whispering outside his door, debating if they should take the knife to the throat and let blood spill. Without a further word, Makarr whispered a spell, ripping the lives of all creatures in the farmstead. Little did he know - the farmer and his wife were wondering if they should slaughter a pig and offer Makarr a feast for the next day in apology for their dispute. Upon examining the farmer's soul, Makarr realized the gravity of his assumptions and for three days and nights turned to despair, cursing their souls to stalk the farmstead for the rest of eternity. Thus was born the Boneskinner; and thus the Weave took notice of Makarr's fractured soul. The First Phylactery was thus created - the farmer's kitchen knife. 
2. The Second Betrayal came soon after. Tortured by what he had done to the humble farmers, Makarr fled east. Here he found himself waylaid by a group of peasants calling out his name. At first, Makarr ignored them and continued on his flight. For two days the peasants followed him, constantly calling to him. On the third day, Makarr had enough, and uttered a spell to freeze the peasants. In his frustration, Makarr amended the spell to freeze their souls to the forest, thus birthing the Black Forest. The Weave was now interested; this second fracture of Makarr's soul now allowed it to whisper into his heart directly. The Second Phylactery was thus created - a humble peasant's walking stick. 
3. The Third Betrayal was the first incited by the Weave. Makarr sat a professorship at the [[Guilds/Arcane University (North)\|Arcane University (North)]] in [[World - Geography - Material Plane/The Imperial Continent/Chrannoth-Uggan (Northern Capitol)\|Chrannoth-Uggan (Northern Capitol)]]. Here he delved deep into mystical mysteries, mediating and researching the fabric of the [[World - Metaphysics/Barrier of Worlds\|Barrier of Worlds]]. Thus was he led to the [[World - Geography - Material Plane/The Imperial Continent/Mirrormere\|Mirrormere]], that crystal pool of dreams. Makarr carved out a maze of tunnels from the very bedrock itself, jealously guarding the pool from any who would seek to walk his path. In his selfishness, Makarr entombed several hundred souls in the passageways to serve as his watchful eyes and ears for aeons to come. This foul ambition was whispered to Makarr by [[World - Metaphysics/The Weave\|The Weave]]. "Let the unworthy prove their worth to you", it said. And so Makarr lured orphans over a decade, tearing the sanctity of his soul even further. the Third phylactery was thus created - an orphan babe's rattle.
