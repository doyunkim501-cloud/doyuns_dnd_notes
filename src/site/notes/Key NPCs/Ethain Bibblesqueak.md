[[World - Races/Gnomes\|Gnomes]], Bard, and professor of histories both arcane and mundane. 

Ethain was commissioned to write [[Lore - Foundatiaonal/Imperial Founding\|Imperial Founding]], an orthodox text of the [[World - Religions/The Eternal Sun/Church of the White Flame (Eternal Flame)\|Church of the White Flame (Eternal Flame)]] some three hundred years after [[World - Religions/The Eternal Sun/The God Emperor (Sol)\|The God Emperor (Sol)]] ascended to [[World - Religions/The Eternal Sun/The Golden Throne\|The Golden Throne]]. (300 Post-Cataclysm)

Ethain was, surprisingly, not a devout follower of the Church. His detractors and naysayers within the [[Guilds/Guild of Lore\|Guild of Lore]] used this against him, claiming he was an agent of the [[World - Religions/Cults/Dark Sun\|Dark Sun]] seeking to overthrow the Emperor's good authority. It was a great surprise to all that Ethain was granted this Divine Commission; and a greater surprise still that he carried it out faithfully. 

On his deathbed, Ethain was said to have professed the Imperial Founding was his greatest work. His last will and testament codified his desire to have it translated into all languages and spread as oral tradition, with no need to emblazon it with his own name. Ethain's funeral procession through [[World - Geography - Material Plane/The Imperial Continent/Chrannoth-Uggan (Northern Capitol)\|Chrannoth-Uggan (Northern Capitol)]] was a large affair, with the day being declared a State Holiday by the [[World - Religions/The Eternal Sun/The Divine Generals\|The Divine Generals]] as they were - one of the highest of honours. 

To this day, Ethain remains celebrated within the [[Guilds/Guild of Lore\|Guild of Lore]] as a seminal member without whom the Guild would not have been granted a position within [[World - Geography - Material Plane/The Imperial Continent/Solaris (Southern Capitol)\|Solaris (Southern Capitol)]].
