[[World - Races/Mindflayers\|Mindflayers]] have created the Dark Sun as their voice in the Above World. 

Since the [[World - Races/Mindflayers\|Mindflayers]] oft treat with [[World - Geography - Material Plane/The Imperial Continent/Solaris (Southern Capitol)\|Solaris (Southern Capitol)]], the Dark Sun have received a rare and valuable State Sanction to perform their activties strictly within Solaris and [[World - Geography - Material Plane/The Imperial Continent/Chrannoth-Uggan (Northern Capitol)\|Chrannoth-Uggan (Northern Capitol)]]. Should any of the [[World - Races/Mindflayers\|Mindflayers]] agents act in capacity as Dark Sun operatives outside these cities, there is a steep price to be paid. 

Within [[World - Geography - Material Plane/The Imperial Continent/The Underdark\|The Underdark]] the Dark Sun is the official ruling body; but all savvy travellers understand that it is the [[World - Races/Mindflayers\|Mindflayers]] who truly run the place. 

Dark Sun mainly operates through [[World - Races/Kobolds\|Kobolds]] and [[World - Races/Goblins\|Goblins]] largely as these Races are already mixed up in illicit activities condemned by [[World - Religions/The Eternal Sun/The Divine Generals\|The Divine Generals]]. 

The Dark Sun's calling sign is a CRESCENT MOON WITHIN A CIRCLE OF FIRE.
